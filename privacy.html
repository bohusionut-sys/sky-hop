Sky Hop - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026 · UK / EU friendly · plain language

Sky Hop (“the app”, “we”) is a free endless flyer game for Android (and a browser demo). This policy explains what information is involved when you play. We do not create user accounts and we do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to play.

Who we are

Sky Hop is published as package com.skyhop.game. Contact for privacy questions: use the email listed on the Google Play store listing for Sky Hop (or the GitHub repository issues for the open web version).

What we store on your device

The game saves progress locally on your device (for example via browser or app storage), such as:

  • High score, coins, Stardust, and unlocks (skins, maps, trails, music)
  • Settings (music mute/volume, vibration, sound effects)
  • Whether you purchased “Remove ads”
  • Optional display name used only for on-device leaderboards
  • Challenge progress and daily reward counters

This data stays on your device unless you clear app data or site storage. We do not operate a Sky Hop login server that receives this save data.

Advertising (AdMob)

The free Android version may show ads (for example interstitial ads between runs, or rewarded ads for optional bonuses such as revive or Stardust). Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC).

Ad networks may collect or process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, IP address, and interaction with ads, in order to show and measure ads and to fight fraud. You may see personalised or non-personalised ads depending on your device settings, region, and consent choices (where required).

  • Google advertising info: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

If you buy Remove ads, we stop showing our game ads. That purchase does not control third-party privacy settings on your device.

In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (for example Remove ads, Stardust packs) are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment details. We only receive purchase confirmation needed to unlock the item on your device. We do not see your full card or bank details.

See Google Play’s terms and privacy information in the Play Store.

No account login

Sky Hop does not require creating an account. Leaderboards and challenges on the device are local. There is no in-game chat and no user-generated social feed.

Children

Sky Hop is a casual arcade game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for Sky Hop accounts (we have none). Parents/guardians can use Google Play family controls and device ad settings. Content rating is intended to reflect mild fantasy obstacle gameplay only.

Legal bases (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

Where these laws apply:

  • Local game saves — necessary to provide the game you asked for (performance of a contract / legitimate interests in running a working game).
  • Ads — where required, based on your consent via the platform/consent form; otherwise as allowed for non-personalised ads and fraud prevention.
  • Purchases — necessary to fulfil the purchase you request through Google Play.

Your choices

  • Clear app storage / site data to reset local progress
  • Use Android advertising ID / privacy settings and Google’s ad settings
  • Skip rewarded ads; play without buying anything
  • Uninstall the app to stop further local processing by Sky Hop

International transfers

Google and other advertising/payment partners may process data in countries outside the UK/EEA. Those providers are responsible for their own transfer safeguards as described in their policies.

Changes

We may update this page when the game’s features or partners change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Web demo

The GitHub Pages web demo uses the same local storage approach. AdMob and Play Billing apply to the Android/Play Store build when those services are enabled.
