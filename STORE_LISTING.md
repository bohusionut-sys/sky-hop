## Full description
Sky Hop is a light endless flyer — tap to hop through scrolling pillars, chase a new best, and unlock a colourful collection of looks.

**Play**
• Tap, click, or press Space / ↑ to flap
• Pass gaps to score; hit a pillar, the ceiling, or the ground and it’s game over
• Best score saves on your device

**Collect & customise**
• Coins from distance flown — unlock Common, Rare & Epic skins, maps, light trails, and music
• Stardust from pipes (and optional packs) — unlock Legendary cosmetics
• Shop tabs: Skins · Maps · Trails · Music · Stardust
• Procedural music vibes — mute and volume on the HUD

**Extra**
• On-device Daily / Weekly / Monthly ranks
• Daily & lifetime challenges with coin / Stardust rewards
• Optional rewarded ads for revive or a little free Stardust
• Remove ads with a one-time purchase

No account required. Progress stays on your device. Optional Google Play purchases for Stardust packs and Remove ads.

Privacy policy: https://bohusionut-sys.github.io/sky-hop/privacy.html

## Content rating notes (IARC / Play questionnaire)
Sky Hop is a single-player flap / endless flyer.
- **Violence:** Mild fantasy / cartoon obstacle collision only (bird hits pillars). No blood, gore, or realistic violence.
- **User-generated content:** None — no chat, no social posting, no player-created levels shared online. On-device leaderboard uses an optional local display name only.
- **Ads:** Yes (AdMob) when not removed via IAP.
- **In-app purchases:** Yes (Remove ads; consumable Stardust packs).
- **Location / personal info sharing:** No Sky Hop account; see privacy policy for ads/billing partners.
- **Target age:** Typically Everyone / PEGI 3-style casual — confirm with the official questionnaire answers.
