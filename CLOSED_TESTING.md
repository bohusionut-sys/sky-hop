# Sky Hop closed testing

Sky Hop is preparing for Google Play production release. On this Play Console account, the closed-testing gate requires **at least 12 opted-in Android testers for about 14 consecutive days** before production access is unlocked. Closed testing gives the developer real-device feedback while satisfying that release requirement.

## How to join

1. Use an Android phone with the Google Play Store and a Google account.
2. Open the closed-test opt-in link: **YOUR_CLOSED_TEST_OPT_IN_URL**
3. Sign in with the Google account you will use on the phone, then choose **Join**.
4. Follow the Play link to install Sky Hop and keep the app installed/available during the test period.

The closed-test link is a placeholder until the closed track is live. The agent will replace `YOUR_CLOSED_TEST_OPT_IN_URL` in these materials once Google Play provides the real opt-in URL.

## What to test

Please play normally and report what happens, including your Android model and Android version where possible:

- Tap to fly, avoid obstacles, restart, and play several runs.
- Confirm ads appear at the expected moments and do not freeze or cover controls.
- Try **Watch for Stardust** and confirm the reward arrives only after a completed rewarded ad.
- Try a revive after a crash and report any problems.
- Browse the shop and try unlocking/equipping character skins, maps, trails, or music with the available currency.
- If you choose to buy remove-ads, check that the purchase completes and ads stay removed. Never feel obligated to buy anything.
- Note crashes, blank screens, stuck loading, missing rewards, touch issues, visual glitches, or anything that feels confusing. A screenshot, device model, Android version, and short reproduction step are especially helpful.

## Feedback

Email feedback to **bohus.ionut@gmail.com**, or open an issue at [github.com/bohusionut-sys/sky-hop/issues](https://github.com/bohusionut-sys/sky-hop/issues). Please say that the report is from closed testing.

## Internal vs closed testing

- **Internal testing** is the small developer-focused track for early build checks. It is useful for quick verification but does **not** replace the closed-testing requirement. Existing internal opt-in: [join internal testing](https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701175694758760911).
- **Closed testing** is the broader tester group that counts toward the production gate: 12+ opted-in testers for about 14 days on this account.
