Sky Hop — Android beta

Help shape an original one-tap flyer with playful skins, maps, and Stardust to collect. Join the Sky Hop tester group and send us the rough edges before launch.

Join the Sky Hop test

  1. Join the Google Group. Join the Sky Hop testers group
    Anyone can join. Member email addresses are visible only to group managers, not to other testers.
  2. Open the closed-testing Play opt-in using the same Google account you used for the group, then follow Google Play's instructions to install Sky Hop: join the closed test on Google Play.

After review, Google Play may take a short while before the closed-test opt-in becomes joinable. Optional early access: join the internal test.

What you need

  • An Android phone or tablet
  • A Google account for Google Play
  • The Play Store installed
