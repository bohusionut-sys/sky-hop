# Sky Hop tester outreach

The live tester-group and closed Play opt-in steps are below. Ask testers to use the **same Google account** for both steps:

1. Join the Google Group: https://groups.google.com/g/skyhop-testers
2. Open the closed-testing Google Play opt-in: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.baigames.skyhop
3. Install Sky Hop and send feedback.

After review, Google Play may take a short while before the closed-test opt-in becomes joinable. Optional early access is available through the internal-testing opt-in: https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701175694758760911

Anyone can join the group. Member email addresses are visible only to group managers, not to other testers. Group email: **skyhop-testers@googlegroups.com**.
